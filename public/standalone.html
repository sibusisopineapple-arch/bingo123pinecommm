School Core Values & Community Policies

Guided by our five pillars: Integrity, Curiosity, Equality, Service, and Community. These principles shape all academic standards, daily campus routines, and interpersonal respect.

The 5 Pillars of USAP Community School

The Five Core Values

Integrity • Curiosity • Equality • Service • Community
Pillar 1

Integrity

“Uncompromising honesty and moral courage”

Doing the right thing even when no one is watching. In all academic assessments, examinations, and community obligations, we uphold truthfulness and honor.

Pillar 2

Curiosity

“Voracious inquiry and intellectual depth”

A passion for questioning assumptions, scientific exploration, and creative problem solving. Scholars interrogate the world with open minds.

Pillar 3

Equality

“Inherent human dignity and mutual respect”

Embracing diversity and upholding fairness. Every individual’s voice and contributions are valued without prejudice across our campus.

Pillar 4

Service

“Empathetic stewardship and servant leadership”

Using intellect and skills to uplift humanity. Exemplified through campus Work Programme stewardship and social impact initiatives.

Pillar 5

Community

“Ubuntu, solidarity, and shared responsibility”

Living as one family where individual growth and community wellbeing are interconnected through Quaker meetings, dining, and daily care.

Mandatory Weekdays

School Uniform Policy

“Students are required to be dressed in complete school uniform during official school hours on all weekdays.”

Full school uniform is compulsory from 7:40 AM through the close of the academic day from Monday to Friday. Neat and tidy presentation is expected across classrooms, labs, and library.

LIFESTYLE PROTOCOL Mandatory
Strict Silence

Silent Evening Prep

“Silent prep must be strictly observed during designated evening study hours in all academic venues and study halls.”

Evening study sessions require absolute quiet to maintain an optimal study environment. Weekday prep concludes at 10:30 PM, while weekend prep concludes at 9:00 PM, after which students retire to dormitories to sleep.

ACADEMIC PROTOCOL Mandatory
Campus Quiet Zone

Exam Quiet Protocol

“Students and staff must maintain complete silence across campus throughout all official examination sessions.”

Whenever an examination or test is in progress, adjacent areas are designated strict quiet zones. Noise disruptions are strictly prohibited across campus.

EXAM PROTOCOL Mandatory
Lunch & Dinner Compulsory

Mandatory Meal Attendance

“Attendance at Lunch and Dinner is compulsory for all students; Breakfast and Snack breaks are optional.”

To safeguard community health, nutrition, and accountability, Lunch (1:00 PM – 2:00 PM) and Dinner (6:00 PM – 7:00 PM) are mandatory. Breakfast and snacks are optional.

COMMUNITY PROTOCOL Mandatory
Campus Stewardship

Student Work Programme Duties

“All students must punctually report to and diligently complete their assigned work programme duties.”

Work programme responsibilities instill stewardship, shared responsibility, and practical leadership across campus. Students report punctually to designated supervisors.

SERVICE PROTOCOL Mandatory
Sunday Fellowship

Sunday Quaker Meeting for Worship

“On Sundays, all community members are expected to gather in the Dining Hall or outdoor grounds for Quaker meeting, unless formally excused on medical grounds.”

Sunday Quaker meeting provides communal reflection and spiritual fellowship. Attendance is expected for all individuals; those unwell must report to the clinic or leadership.
